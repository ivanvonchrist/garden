# Garden / The Mouth of Elsewhere

A procedural organism occupying a breach in causality. Move your pointer to fold it, click to feed it glyphs, and repeal seven laws of reality to alter its geometry, color, telemetry, and optional synthesized sound. Keyboard users can feed and mutate it using the buttons. Nothing is stored or sent. No dependencies or API keys.

## Local

Node.js 20 or newer. Run `npm run dev` and open http://127.0.0.1:3000. Run `npm run build` to generate `dist`.

## Vercel

Import `ivanvonchrist/garden`, select **Other** as the framework, use `npm run build` as the build command and `dist` as the output directory, then deploy. `vercel.json` configures these automatically. No environment variables needed. Connected pushes to main deploy automatically.

Alternatively: `npx vercel` for a preview and `npx vercel --prod` for production.

Animation is capped at 30 frames per second, stops in hidden tabs, and starts still for reduced-motion visitors. The stillness button pauses it at any time. Audio requires explicit activation. Optional Google Fonts have local fallbacks.

Iteration 3 adds a detachable time organ: scrub a bounded history of 240 states and fossilize up to seven outlines. Fossils remain only for this visit and resize with the viewport.

Iteration 4: arm the wound tool and drag across the canvas to divide the mesh and grow a second mouth. Divide without a hand offers the same ritual via keyboard; Heal reunites the body. Time history now restores divisions as well as geometry.

Iteration 5 completes the encounter: Be swallowed enters a second scene with five draggable organs. Arrow keys move a focused organ; their horizontal order composes the body's belief. Escape or Return exits. Iterations continue every ten minutes until explicitly stopped by the user.

Iteration 6 connects the interior and exterior: Transplant saves the organ arrangement for this visit, changes the outer membrane's shape, reveals anatomical anchor lines, and retunes active sound.

Iteration 7 adds a negative sun: summon it to gravitationally distort the outer mesh. Move over the canvas to relocate it, or focus its button and use arrow keys. Dismiss it to restore the undistorted geometry.

Iteration 8: convene the unborn parliament and vote an organ out of existence. Its interior connections disappear, its ghost remains movable, the body's sentence changes, and the exterior membrane develops an organ-specific deformation. Rescind restores all five.

Iteration 9 adds an unfaithful mirror. Capture the membrane's current state, change its laws or anatomy, and blend the living folds with the frozen double using a keyboard-accessible slider. Shattering releases the snapshot. One small snapshot is retained only for the visit.

Iteration 10 adds forbidden names: a transient word becomes a deterministic geometry and frequency signature, then is cleared. The ritual cabinet consolidates secondary controls into a scrollable drawer on desktop and mobile. No word is saved or transmitted.

Iteration 11 turns the body into an aperture: moving waves occupy its exterior while a bounded star field appears through its missing flesh. Forbidden names alter the aperture's teeth, laws alter its outline, and the negative sun warps its boundary. Toggle the ritual again to restore skin.

Iteration 12 lets visitors borrow a projected body 1–60 seconds ahead. A dashed spectral membrane predicts the current geometry under the active laws, anatomy, mirror, and negative sun. Repay advances simulation time to that body; cancel dismisses it. Only one future body is rendered, with a reduced mesh.

Iteration 13 adds three contradictory spaces: returning seams, reversed returns, and dislocated islands. The mesh and borrowed future body wrap around a visible chart, with strokes broken at seams rather than drawing accidental bridges. Cycle the ritual a fourth time to restore ordinary space.

Iteration 14 reverses observation: the creature's surviving organs vote to classify the visitor. Organ positions and forbidden-name signatures determine votes. The winning law and a contradictory topology are imposed together; appeal restores the earlier law and topology. Witness testimony explains each vote.
